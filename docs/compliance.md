# Rule checks for a permit to work system

Checked 30 October 2026. Run `npm run permits -- compliance` (or `/compliance`, `/start-of-shift`). These checks find permits, isolations, people and companies whose records do not match the rules below. They check what is recorded, not the plant, the atmosphere or the people, and they do not replace your site's permit to work procedure, a competent person's judgement or legal advice. The full standards (AS/NZS 2865, AS 1674.1, AS/NZS 4836) are sold by Standards New Zealand and Standards Australia.

The duty behind every check: in New Zealand, the primary duty of care in the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, s 36, to ensure so far as is reasonably practicable the health and safety of workers, including safe plant, safe systems of work and the information, training, instruction and supervision they need (s 36(3)). In Australia, the same primary duty in the model Work Health and Safety Act 2011, s 19, as adopted in each state. [HSWA 2015 on legislation.govt.nz](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2015/0070/latest/DLM5976660.html), [Safe Work Australia model WHS laws](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation/model-whs-laws)

| Check | What the data shows | Rule and source | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| permit-expired | An issued permit past its valid-to time. | Site permit to work procedure: work stops when the permit runs out, and goes on only under a new or revalidated permit. | The site sets each kind's longest validity in `ptw.permit_kinds.max_hours`. |
| permit-too-long | An open permit issued for longer than its kind allows. | Site permit to work procedure (`max_hours`). | A warning. `request` refuses new ones. |
| isolation-not-verified | An issued permit that needs isolation has no linked isolation, or the linked one was never proved dead. | Isolate, lock, then prove zero energy before work: WorkSafe NZ, Safe use of machinery (isolation and lockout); Safe Work Australia Code of Practice, Managing the risks of plant in the workplace; for electrical work, test before touch under AS/NZS 4836 (safe working on or near low-voltage electrical installations). [WorkSafe NZ machinery guidance](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/machinery/), [Safe Work Australia plant code](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/doc/model-code-practice-managing-risks-plant-workplace) | Reads the verify record. It does not know whether the test was done well. |
| isolation-incomplete | An isolation locks fewer points than the asset has. | Same sources: every energy source on the plant is isolated, including stored energy (gravity, pressure, product). | Only as good as the asset's isolation point list, which the site owns. |
| lock-left-on | A personal lock still on an isolation by someone not signed on to an open permit that relies on it. | Site lockout procedure: each worker puts on and takes off their own lock. | A lock left on is a sign someone may think the plant is still safe, or left without signing off. |
| isolation-orphaned | An isolation still in place with no open permit relying on it. | Site isolation procedure. | A warning: remove it, or link the next permit. |
| gas-test-missing, gas-test-stale, gas-test-failed | An issued permit that needs a gas test has none, the last one is older than `gas_retest_minutes`, or a reading is outside the limits. | Confined spaces: oxygen between 19.5% and 23.5%, and flammable gas below 5% of its lower explosive limit, for entry; atmosphere tested by a competent person and monitored while people are inside. AS/NZS 2865:2009 Confined spaces; Safe Work Australia Code of Practice, Confined spaces; WorkSafe NZ confined spaces guidance. [Safe Work Australia confined spaces code](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/doc/model-code-practice-confined-spaces), [WorkSafe NZ confined spaces](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/confined-spaces/) | The oxygen and flammable gas limits are seeded from those sources. The hydrogen sulphide and carbon monoxide limits are the operator's: check them against the current workplace exposure standard before relying on them. Limits live in `ptw.gas_limits` with their source. |
| standby-missing | An issued confined space permit with no standby person or no rescue plan. | A standby person outside the space in contact with those inside, and emergency and rescue procedures in place before entry: AS/NZS 2865:2009; Safe Work Australia Code of Practice, Confined spaces. | Checks the record only. It does not check the rescue gear is there. |
| entry-not-permitted | Someone recorded inside on a permit that is suspended or no longer issued. | Same sources: entry only under a current permit. | |
| ticket-missing, ticket-expired | A person on an open permit (issuer, receiver, crew, standby, gas tester or isolator) without a current ticket for that role. | Training and competence for the work: HSWA 2015 s 36(3)(f); WHS Act 2011 s 19(3)(f). Ticket kinds and their sources are in `ptw.ticket_kinds`. | Which ticket each role needs is the site's choice, recorded in `ptw.permit_kinds.crew_ticket` and the role list in the `role_needs` view. |
| contractor-induction | A contractor on an open permit with no site induction, or a lapsed one. | Information and training for everyone at the workplace: HSWA 2015 s 36(3)(f); WHS Act 2011 s 19(3)(f). | |
| contractor-insurance | A contractor company with people on an open permit and no current insurance certificate on file. | Operator policy in this base, common in contractor management. | Change it in a migration if your policy differs. |
| permit-conflict | Two issued permits that clash by `ptw.conflict_rules` (hot work and a confined space entry in one area, lifting over work at height, two crews on one asset). | Coordinating work between duty holders and crews on one site: HSWA 2015 s 34 (consult, cooperate and coordinate with other PCBUs); WHS Act 2011 s 46. | The clash list is the site's. Add pairs with /customise. |
| fire-watch-short | Hot work closed in the last 30 days with no fire watch recorded, or one shorter than `fire_watch_minutes` (60 by default). | AS 1674.1 Safety in welding and allied processes, Part 1: fire precautions, and your insurer's hot work conditions. | The length is the operator's setting. Check your insurer's wording. |
| handover-missing | An open permit with no shift handover for longer than `shift_hours`. | Site permit to work procedure: a permit that runs past a shift is handed over and the new receiver accepts it. | A warning. |

The commands enforce the same rules as gates: `issue` and `resume` refuse until isolation, gas, standby, receiver ticket, contractor and clash checks pass; `sign-on` refuses a person without the crew ticket or a contractor without induction and insurance; `enter` refuses without a fresh passing gas test and a standby; `deisolate` refuses while a permit relies on it or a personal lock is on; `close` refuses while anyone is inside. Each gate can be passed only with `--override-by="<name>"`, and the acceptance is written on the permit.

To clear a finding, fix the work or the record: retest, lock the missing point, prove it dead, renew the ticket, induct the contractor, suspend one of two clashing permits, record the handover. Never edit a rule to make a finding disappear. If a rule here is out of date, update this file and the check in a new migration together, with the date and source.

Not checked here: the quality of the risk assessment, the condition of locks, gas monitors and rescue gear, monitor calibration dates, hazardous area (explosive dust) zoning, notifiable event reporting to WorkSafe or the state regulator, asbestos and radiation permits, and state-by-state differences in Australian high risk work licences. Those need their own records.
